01

The case: factories that buy far away

Cikarang, in Bekasi Regency, West Java, is home to some of Indonesia’s largest industrial estates. Many of its manufacturers still rely on virgin materials and imported industrial inputs.

9Industry, innovation and infrastructure
SDG focus

Goal 9: Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization and foster innovation

The competition supports target 9.4 (making industries more resource-efficient with cleaner technologies) and target 9.3 (bringing small, local enterprises into industrial value chains). Mapping local suppliers and recyclers gives Cikarang’s manufacturers a practical way to do both.

03

Who can join and what to build

Eligibility

  • University students and young innovators aged 18–30.
  • Teams of 3–5 people; cross-university teams are welcome.
  • At least one member handles data or programming, and at least one handles design or field research.
  • Registration is free.

Required platform features

  • Supplier profiles: name, location, raw materials, product specifications and availability.
  • Search and filters by material type, distance and availability.
  • A map view of all listed suppliers.
  • A “verified” mark for suppliers your team has confirmed.
  • A way for manufacturers to send an inquiry to a supplier.

Ground rules

  • List at least 30 verified suppliers in Cikarang and the surrounding area.
  • Get each supplier’s consent before publishing their contact details.
  • Use open data and open-source tools where possible; credit every data source.
  • All work must be original and built during the competition.

07

FAQ

Do we need to visit suppliers in person?

Not always. A supplier counts as verified when your team confirms its details directly, by visit, phone or video call, and the supplier agrees to be listed.

Which areas count as “Cikarang and surrounding areas”?

Cikarang and the wider Bekasi Regency, plus nearby Karawang and Bekasi City. Suppliers further away can be listed but do not count toward the 30.

Can we use AI tools or existing code libraries?

Yes, if you name them in your report. The supplier data and the platform design must be your team’s own work.

Who owns the platform after the competition?

Teams keep ownership of their code. The winning team agrees to keep the platform online for at least 12 months with launch support from the organizers.

How is the impact measured after launch?

See “Impact review” in the competition plan above: listed suppliers, manufacturer reach, inquiries sent and a short manufacturer survey, compared before and after launch.
